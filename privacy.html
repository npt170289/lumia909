Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Lumia 909 is a restaurant and venue information application providing menu browsing, automated menu assistance, booking requests, and Pi-related Testnet functionality.

Information We May Process

Depending on your use of the application, we may process:

  • Pi account information, such as username or UID, when you authenticate through Pi.
  • Booking information voluntarily submitted through the booking form, such as name, contact details, requested date and time, party size, and booking notes.
  • Questions or messages submitted to the chatbot.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the application.

Purposes

Information is used to display menus and venue information, manage booking requests, answer restaurant-related questions, support Pi authentication and testing, maintain security, and respond to inquiries.

Booking Infrastructure

The booking form uses Supabase infrastructure shared with the Garden Station 303 project. Information submitted through Lumia 909 may therefore be processed or stored using that shared infrastructure for booking management.

Chatbot

The chatbot provides automated information about the menu and venue. Responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not submit passwords, wallet private keys, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Pi Testnet

Pi Testnet features are provided for testing. Test-Pi is not represented as real-money payment or legal tender. Testnet transactions may be recorded on a public blockchain.

Sharing, Retention, and Rights

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information as necessary to operate the application. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes or as required by law.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.
